Thanksgiving family games for all ages

The best Thanksgiving games need almost no setup, work for a 6-year-old and a grandparent, and give everyone something to cheer about. Here are ideas for every part of the day.

At the table (no prep)

  • Thankful alphabet: go around the table naming something you're thankful for, A to Z. Stuck? You're out.
  • Two truths and a turkey: two true facts and one made-up one; everyone guesses the fake.
  • Pass the question: write questions on slips ("best family trip?"), put them in a bowl, and pass it around.

After dinner: get everyone moving

  • Cup stack race: fastest to stack and unstack a pyramid of plastic cups.
  • Balloon keep-up: a team keeps a balloon off the floor using only elbows and knees.
  • The living-room showdown: put an active game on the TV and run a knockout. Everyone takes a turn, the lowest score sits out each round, and the last player standing wins bragging rights until next year.

How to run a TV showdown with just a phone

  1. Mirror a phone to the TV (how to mirror your phone).
  2. Prop the phone up waist high and clear 6 to 8 feet of floor.
  3. Pick teams: kids vs grown-ups, or each household vs the others.
  4. Everyone plays one round; add up team scores or play knockout.
