Family games you can play on the TV with just a phone

You don't need a game console to turn the TV into family game night. Most phones can show their screen on a TV, and phone cameras are now good enough to follow your whole body. That means active, everyone-can-play games with nothing extra to buy.

What you need

  • A phone (iPhone or Android) and a way to show it on the TV: AirPlay or Screen Mirroring on iPhone, Cast / Screen Cast / Smart View on Android, or an HDMI adapter for either.
  • Something to prop the phone on, about waist high, facing the room. A small phone tripod is ideal.
  • About 6 to 8 feet of clear floor so the player is in view head to toe.

Not sure how to get your phone on the TV? See how to mirror your phone to the TV for games.

Why active TV games work for families

  • Everyone can play. No controller skills needed: if you can punch, duck or jump, you can play.
  • Taking turns is half the fun. One person plays while the couch cheers, coaches and heckles.
  • It's easy to set up anywhere. Grandma's house, a cousin's living room, a holiday party: bring a phone and you're done.

Game night ideas

  • Knockout: everyone plays one round; lowest score sits out until one champion is left.
  • Kids vs grown-ups: add up each team's scores. Handicap the adults with the hardest level.
  • Beat the high score: post a score on the fridge and let anyone challenge it all weekend.
  • Phone vs phone: if everyone has a phone on the same Wi-Fi, play the same round at the same time.
