How to mirror your phone to the TV for games

Mirroring shows exactly what's on your phone on the big screen, so the whole room can watch. Here's how to do it on iPhone and Android, wirelessly or with a cable.

iPhone

Wireless: AirPlay / Screen Mirroring

  1. Make sure the iPhone and the TV (or Apple TV) are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner).
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring and pick your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV. Many recent Samsung, LG, Sony, Vizio and Roku TVs support AirPlay.
  4. If a code appears on the TV, type it on the phone.

With a cable

Use an HDMI adapter: Lightning to HDMI for older iPhones, or USB-C to HDMI for iPhone 15 and later. Plug in, and the screen mirrors right away. A cable has the least delay.

Android

Wireless: Cast, Screen Cast or Smart View

  1. Put the phone and the TV (or Chromecast / Google TV) on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Swipe down twice to open Quick Settings.
  3. Tap Cast or Screen Cast (Pixel and most phones) or Smart View (Samsung), then choose the TV.

Don't see the tile? Tap the pencil icon in Quick Settings to add it, or use the Google Home app: tap your Chromecast, then Cast my screen.

With a cable

Many USB-C Android phones (including recent Samsung Galaxy S models) output video through a USB-C to HDMI adapter. Check your phone's specs for "DisplayPort over USB-C".

Tips for games

  • Turn on the TV's Game Mode to cut picture processing delay.
  • Keep the router close, or use a cable if the picture stutters.
  • Turn landscape rotation on so the game fills the screen.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications don't pop up on the TV.
  • Prop the phone up where it can see the player. With motion games, the phone is the camera and the TV is the screen.
