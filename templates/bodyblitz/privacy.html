Body Blitz privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Body Blitz is made by {{company}}. It tracks your movement on your phone. We don't run servers for the game, we don't have accounts, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The camera is used live to track your body. Video is not recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Body tracking data. The positions of points on your body, used during play and not kept.
  • Players and scores. Names and heights you enter, teams, scores, trophies and settings.

Deleting the app removes what it stored.

Diagnostics from Google ML Kit

Body Blitz uses Google's ML Kit to detect body position on the device. ML Kit sends Google limited diagnostic information so Google can keep the feature working, such as device type, OS version, app version, performance data and a per-installation identifier. It does not send your video. See Google's ML Kit documentation for details.

Purchases

The unlock is sold by Apple or Google, who handle payment under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your payment details. The game asks the store whether your account owns the unlock, on your phone.

Children

Body Blitz isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's agreement and supervision.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
