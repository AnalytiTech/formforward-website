Do I have to pay again if I delete the game or get a new phone?

No. The unlock belongs to the Apple or Google account that bought it. Reinstall while signed in to that account and it should unlock by itself. If it doesn't, go to Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds. Request one through Apple or Google Play.

The game can't see me

Prop the phone up in landscape, step back until your whole body is in view, and make sure the room is well lit. The game's voice guides you into position.

Health and safety

Body Blitz is physically demanding. Only play if you know you are healthy enough for vigorous exercise; you are responsible for your own health and safety. Read the full information in our terms of use.
