How to play

Prop up your phone

Lean it sideways on something steady, about waist high. A small phone tripod makes this easy.

Step back

About 6 to 8 feet, so the camera sees you head to toe. The game's voice tells you when you're in the right spot.

Move to play

Hit every move as it comes. Fast reactions score more, and combos build your multiplier.

What's inside

Three levels

  • Warm Up: learn the moves (free)
  • Blitz: every move, faster
  • Overdrive: far moves, no rest

Plus Progressive, where each round gets harder until you drop.

Play together

Take turns in knockout rounds with a rising score bar. Play solo, as a family, or team against team, with saved players, a leaderboard and trophies.

Private by design

Tracking runs on your phone. Your video is never recorded or uploaded, and there's no account to make. Privacy policy

Price

Warm Up is free, so you can check the game works in your space. One payment unlocks Blitz, Overdrive, Progressive and every multiplayer game. No subscription and no ads.

Health and safety

Body Blitz is a workout

It's physically demanding and can challenge you physically. Only play if you know you're healthy enough for vigorous exercise; you're responsible for your own health and safety. Clear your space, and stop if anything hurts. Players under 18 need a parent or guardian's agreement and supervision.
