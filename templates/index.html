Hands-free sessions

Set it on a tripod and keep shooting

Continuous sessions capture rep after rep while you practice: ten jump shots, twenty swings, a bucket of serves. Afterwards you get a report on the whole set.

  • Average and best score, and a bar for every rep.
  • Consistency: how steady each measurement stayed across the set.
  • The one thing to focus on, from the measurement most often off target.

Yoga, mobility and rehab

Range-of-motion checks record the angle you reach, for shoulder, elbow, hip, knee and neck, and track it over time. Programs by body area guide you through each exercise, with form checks as gentle as you need.

Questions

Do I need any equipment or sensors?

No. Just your phone and somewhere to prop it up, 8–12 feet away with your whole body in view. A cheap phone tripod helps.

Does it track the ball?

It tracks you: 17 points on your body, many times a second. Dribbling, cradling and touches are measured from how your hands and feet move. Object detection (beta), which finds a club, bat or stick in your hands, can be turned on in Settings.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your phone. Recordings stay on the device unless you share them.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (iOS 15.5 or later) and Android. Newer phones track faster in live view; recordings are analyzed in full detail on any supported phone.

Can I use it for physical therapy?

Yes, as a practice and tracking aid for the exercises your therapist recommends. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything.
