How it works

1

Coach requests

Pick Corning or Painted Post, the field, the date and a start and end time. The app flags any clash with an approved booking before it's sent.

2

Director approves

Requests land in one list for the director. Approve or reject in a tap, and the calendar updates for everyone.

3

Everyone sees it

Month and week views of every field. No more group-text schedules or two teams showing up for the same diamond.

Built around league roles

Parents

See when and where your player's team practices and plays, for every field in the league.

Coaches

Request field time, check the calendar before you plan practice, and cancel a booking you no longer need.

Directors

Approve or reject requests, manage reservations, and set who in the league is a parent, coach or director.
