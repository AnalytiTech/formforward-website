Contact form privacy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

{{company}} ("we") runs the intake form on analytitech.net and the contact form on our Facebook page. This page explains what happens to what you send us there.

What we collect

Only what you type in: your name, email, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, business name and a description of what you'd like help with.

How we use it

To reply to you about your project. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell or share your details with anyone.

How it reaches us

Website form answers are delivered to our email inbox by a form service (Web3Forms). Facebook form answers are delivered through Meta's tools for Page owners. Those services handle the data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your details

Email us and we'll delete your messages and any notes about your request.
