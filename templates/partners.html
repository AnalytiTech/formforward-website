Coaches and teams

Hands-free sessions capture a whole set of shots or reps and report consistency across them. Athletes practice at home with the same measurements you use at practice: jump shots, dribbling, defensive stance, swings, throws, shuffles.

Golf pros

Swing and putting analysis your students can do between lessons: tempo, lead arm, turn, head movement, and the 2:1 putting rhythm. They show up to the next lesson with the numbers.

Physical therapy and athletic training

Patients practice their home program with rep counting, gentle form checks and range-of-motion checks they can track over time. No accounts or uploads: patient video stays on the patient's phone.
