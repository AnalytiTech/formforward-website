Goal Line Stand privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026.

Short version: Goal Line Stand, a game by {{company}}, does not collect, store or share any personal information. It works offline and has no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't connect to our servers. We don't run any servers for the game.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (games played, stars earned, upgrades bought, settings, and which purchases you own) is saved only on your device. We never see it. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's own backup (iCloud, Google or a computer backup) if you use one, which Apple, Google or you control. Deleting the game deletes it from the device.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as the football season unlock, are sold through Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play on Android. Apple or Google processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy or Google's privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple or Google account information. The game asks the store, on your device, whether your account owns an unlock. On iPhone and iPad, if Family Sharing is on, Apple shares purchases with your family group.

Statistics from Apple and Google

Apple and Google give app developers aggregated, anonymous statistics, such as how many people installed the game, which device models and system versions it runs on, and crash counts. On iPhone and iPad this depends on whether you chose to share analytics with app developers in your settings. The stores prepare these reports; they don't identify you, and the game itself sends us nothing.

Children

Goal Line Stand is rated for ages 9 and up on the App Store and isn't in the App Store's Kids category or Google Play's Teacher Approved program. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Purchases on a child's device follow the store's parental controls, such as Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time or Google Family Link purchase approvals.

Links

The game's Settings screen links to this page and to our support page. They open in your web browser, outside the game.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We don't hold any: everything the game stores stays on your device, and you can delete it by deleting the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You can also contact your local privacy regulator.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting any information, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and change the date above.
