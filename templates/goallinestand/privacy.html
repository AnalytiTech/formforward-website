Goal Line Stand privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Short version: Goal Line Stand, a game by {{company}}, does not collect, store or share any personal information. It works offline and has no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't connect to our servers. We don't run any servers for the game.

What stays on your iPhone

Your game progress (games played, stars earned, upgrades bought, settings, and which purchases you own) is saved only on your device. We never see it. Like other app data, it's included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup if you use one, which Apple or you control. Deleting the game deletes it from the device.

Purchases

In-app purchases, such as the football season unlock, are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account information. The game asks the App Store, on your device, whether your account owns an unlock. If Family Sharing is on, Apple shares purchases with your family group.

Statistics from Apple

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as how many people installed the game or which iOS versions it runs on. Apple prepares these reports; they don't identify you, and the game itself sends us nothing.

Children

Goal Line Stand is rated for ages 9 and up and isn't in the App Store's Kids category. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Purchases on a child's device follow Apple's parental controls, such as Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Links

The game's Settings screen links to this page and to our support page. They open in your web browser, outside the game.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We don't hold any: everything the game stores stays on your iPhone, and you can delete it by deleting the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You can also contact your local privacy regulator.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting any information, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and change the date above.
