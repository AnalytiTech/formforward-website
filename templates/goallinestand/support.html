Goal Line Stand: help & support

Email {{supportEmail}} with your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap an empty pad on the field to put a player on it: QB, Kicker, Coach or O-Line. The first tap shows their range, and a second tap confirms. Press Kick Off to start the wave. Stopping rivals earns cash for more players between waves. Tap the field to move your Captain. Use Hail Mary and Sub In when the rivals get close.

I'm stuck on a game

  • Mix your team. Kickers splash crowds and crack armor, QBs pick off fast runners, and Coaches make nearby players faster.
  • Tap a player to Go Pro. Pros hit much harder.
  • Spend your stars at Training Camp. Replay earlier games for 3 stars to earn more.

What's free?

The first 3 games are free. One purchase unlocks all 14 football games. In the game it's called the Full Game; in the App Store it's listed as Football: Full Season. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else to buy for football.

I bought the unlock but the games are locked

Open Settings → Restore Purchase in the game while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. On a child's device with Ask to Buy, the games unlock as soon as the parent approves. If Family Sharing is on, family members can restore it the same way.

Do I have to pay again on a new iPhone?

No. The unlock belongs to your Apple Account. Install the game while signed in to that account and tap Restore Purchase. Your game progress is saved on the device, so it comes along only if you set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
