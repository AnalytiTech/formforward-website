Goal Line Stand: help & support

Email support@analytitech.net with your phone or tablet model, its iOS, iPadOS or Android version and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

Tap an empty pad on the field to put a player on it: QB, Kicker, Coach or O-Line. The first tap shows their range, and a second tap confirms. Press Kick Off to start the wave. Stopping rivals earns cash for more players between waves. Tap the field to move your Captain. Use Hail Mary and Sub In when the rivals get close.

I'm stuck on a game

  • Mix your team. Kickers splash crowds and crack armor, QBs pick off fast runners, and Coaches make nearby players faster.
  • Tap a player to Go Pro. Pros hit much harder.
  • Spend your stars at Training Camp. Replay earlier games for 3 stars to earn more.

What's free?

The first 3 games are free. One purchase, Football: Full Season, unlocks all 14 football games. More sports are coming soon; for now football is the only one, and there are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing else to buy for football.

I bought Football: Full Season but the games are locked

Open Settings → Restore Purchase in the game while signed in with the Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. On a child's device with Ask to Buy or Family Link approvals, the games unlock as soon as the parent approves. On iPhone and iPad, if Family Sharing is on, family members can restore it the same way.

Do I have to pay again on a new device?

No. The unlock belongs to your Apple Account or Google account, on the same kind of device you bought it on (iPhone and iPad, or Android). Install the game while signed in to that account and tap Restore Purchase. Your game progress is saved on the device, so it comes along only if you set up the new device from a backup of the old one.

Refunds

The store handles refunds. For the App Store, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, see Google Play refunds.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
